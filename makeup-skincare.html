Makeup & Skincare Guide

How to actually use what you already own, plus the basics behind every recommendation.

Your Products

Rare Beauty Soft Pinch Liquid Blush — Hope

Cream blush · Buildable · Dewy finish

  • How much: Start with 1-2 small dots per cheek — this formula is pigmented and builds fast. Easier to add more than to take away.
  • How to apply: Dot onto the apples of your cheeks (smile to find them), then blend outward toward your temples using clean fingertips or a damp makeup sponge. Fingertips give the most natural, your-skin-but-better look.
  • When: Apply after any base/concealer, before setting powder if you use one. On bare skin, it works beautifully applied last.
  • Tip: Warm the product between your fingers for a few seconds before applying — it blends more smoothly and evenly that way.

Clinique Almost Lipstick — Pink Honey

Sheer tint · Buildable · Balm-like finish

  • How to apply: Straight from the bullet for a quick sheer wash of color, or dab on with a fingertip for an even softer, your-lips-but-better look.
  • Building color: One layer gives a natural tint; two layers gives noticeably more pigment without ever looking heavy.
  • Comfort: This formula is moisturizing, so it works well solo with zero other lip products — genuinely low-effort.

TONYMOLY Mask Melt Firming Overnight Mask

Overnight treatment · Firming

  • When: Last step of an evening routine, after cleansing and any serum/moisturizer.
  • How to apply: A thin, even layer over the face, avoiding the eye area. Leave on overnight; rinse or gently tissue off in the morning depending on how your skin feels.
  • Frequency: 2-3 nights a week is a reasonable starting point rather than every night, especially at first.
  • Before first use: Patch test on the inner arm and wait 24 hours, since it's a leave-on overnight formula and reactions are easier to catch before they're on your face.

TONYMOLY Mask Melt Brightening Overnight Mask

Overnight treatment · Brightening

  • When: Same last-step-of-the-night placement as the firming mask above.
  • Pairing with the firming mask: Alternate nights rather than layering both the same night, at least until you know how your skin responds to each on its own.
  • Marketing vs. reality: "Brightening" here means evening out tone and adding glow over time with consistent use — not a dramatic overnight change, and not a substitute for sun protection during the day.
  • Before first use: Same patch-test advice as above — inner arm, 24 hours.

e.l.f. Vegan Bubble Gum & Cherry Lip Balm Duo

Everyday lip balm · Tinted

  • How to use: Apply anytime for hydration alone, or underneath the Clinique lipstick for extra comfort on drier days.
  • Best for: No-makeup days, reapplying through the day, or as the very last step to add a touch of shine over other lip color.

General Application Order

For the minimal, natural looks this app suggests — skincare always comes first, and each step should be fully absorbed before the next.

  1. Skincare prep (cleanse, treat, moisturize)
  2. Base or concealer, only where genuinely needed
  3. Blush
  4. Lip color
  5. Eyes, if doing anything beyond mascara
  6. Setting spray or powder, only if the day is long or humid

Everyday Skincare Basics

Morning

Cleanse → lightweight moisturizer → SPF. That's genuinely enough most days — more steps aren't automatically better.

Evening

Cleanse → any treatment step (like an overnight mask, a few nights a week) → moisturizer if not already covered by the treatment.

Worth knowing: Patch test anything new on the inner arm before applying to your face, especially overnight formulas. This guide is general practical advice, not a diagnosis or medical recommendation. If you're dealing with anything persistent, painful, spreading, or that just doesn't feel right, a dermatologist is always the better call than guessing from an app — including this one.
